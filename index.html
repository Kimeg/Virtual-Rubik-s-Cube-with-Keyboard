Help
  • Drag the cube to rotate the view; mouse wheel zooms. The View key (default W) flips to the mirror view across the YZ plane.
  • Cube colors set the color of each side as the cube was built, so the colors follow the pieces when you turn. Body is the black plastic between the tiles.
  • Key bindings: click a key button, press a letter, then Save. Every key must be unique. x/y/z rotate the whole cube like R/U/F.
  • M/E/S are middle slices (on even cubes they use one central layer). Auto-solve reverses moves made in this simulator.
  • Replay: first line is the scramble, following lines are steps; text after // is a label. Moves: U D L R F B, wide r / Rw, M E S, x y z, with ' and 2.
  • CFOP training: switch the mode at the top right, choose Cross, F2L, OLL or PLL, and click an algorithm. It is demonstrated move by move with the cross color on the bottom; use the controls under the cube to play, stop, resume, or step forward and backward.
  • While a replay is loaded, manual key turns are disabled; press Exit replay to turn the cube yourself.
